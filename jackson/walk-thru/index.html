Why Locumfy exists

Medical recruiting runs on scattered data and manual work.

The talent is there and the demand is there — but the tools connecting them are fragmented. Locumfy is designed to close the gaps that cost recruiters time and margin.

◧

Fragmented brands

Candidates are split across profession-specific job boards. There is no single, branded home where a nurse, therapist, or physician builds a lasting profile and stays engaged.

✎

Manual resume work

Recruiters reformat and re-key resumes by hand for every facility submission — slow, inconsistent, and error-prone, especially when personal details must be removed.

◔

No lifecycle

Most tools stop at the job board. Finding the role, verifying credentials, tracking hours, and getting paid all happen in separate systems that never talk to each other.

The opportunity: a platform that augments — rather than replaces — the existing brand strategy, giving every profession a shared home while making the day-to-day recruiting workflow faster and more automated.
The big picture

One platform, two audiences, connected by a smart data engine.

Think of Locumfy as a marketplace with a brain. Medical professionals use a mobile app; facilities and recruiters use a web portal. Behind both sits an engine that turns raw resumes and public records into rich, verified, searchable profiles.

◎

A shared home

Every professional builds one profile that follows them across roles and years — not a throwaway application for a single job.

⚡

A live marketplace

Facilities post work, professionals apply, and the platform tracks each opportunity from first interest all the way through to payment.

✓

Built-in trust

Profiles are cross-checked against official records, so a facility can trust that a license, specialty, or credential is real before reaching out.

Who uses it

The same platform, tailored to each side of the market.

Professionals and facilities see different tools designed for what they each need — but they share one network, one messaging system, and one set of verified profiles.

End to end

From a resume to a paycheck — one continuous path.

Most tools only cover one slice of this. Locumfy's advantage is that a single professional profile carries all the way through the workflow, and every step feeds the next.

A closer look: how an application actually works

The heart of the marketplace is a simple, fair set of rules that keeps both sides in control. Behind the scenes it works like a set of stop-lights:

A professional applies to a job

The application is now live and visible to the facility that posted the role. The professional can withdraw at any time before being accepted.

The facility accepts or declines

When a facility accepts, that professional is locked to the role — and their other open applications are automatically set aside, so nobody is double-booked.

The work happens and hours are logged

Once accepted, the professional records timesheets against the assignment. Only the facility that posted the job can approve those hours.

The job is completed and closed

The facility marks the assignment complete. Approved hours become the basis for billing and payment — closing the loop from application to paycheck.

Why this matters for a recruiter: every stage is captured as structured data, so time-to-fill, applicant funnels, and hours worked are measurable automatically — no spreadsheets, no chasing paperwork.
The differentiator

The resume engine is what makes Locumfy hard to copy.

Any team can build a job board. What sets Locumfy apart is the technology that reads a resume, structures it, verifies it against official records, and can re-format it on demand — the exact manual work recruiters do today, done automatically.

⌾

Instant onboarding

A professional drops in a resume and their whole profile fills itself in — no typing 18 sections by hand. This is the flow that turns a seeded contact into an active user.

✓

Verified credentials

Profiles are cross-checked against official healthcare records, so licenses and identifiers carry real trust — a genuinely medical-specific advantage.

🛈

Anonymized submissions

Generate a clean, standardized resume with personal details stripped out for third-party submission — the industry-standard recruiting task, automated.

In plain terms: the work a recruiter does by hand for every candidate — reformatting, removing personal details, double-checking credentials — becomes a button.
The network effect

Not just a job board — a living professional community.

Because Locumfy understands who worked where, studied where, and shares specialties, it can suggest meaningful professional connections — the way LinkedIn does, but specific to healthcare. That drives the daily engagement a job board can't.

Connections that make sense

The platform's research projected 660,000 likely professional connections from a study set of 45,000 profiles — relationships inferred from shared employers, schools, specialties, and geography.

  • Grow — suggested peers a professional is likely to know
  • Feed — career-specific news, posts, and updates worth returning for
  • Messaging — direct conversations between professionals and facilities

This is what converts a one-time job seeker into a recurring, loyal user — and keeps candidates warm between assignments.

Seeded, not cold: because the network is pre-loaded from existing resumes, professionals arrive to find their peers and relevant jobs already present — avoiding the empty "cold start" that sinks most new networks.

Where it's going

A de-risked platform with a clear path forward.

The core is already built — mobile app, web portal, and the resume engine. The roadmap is about hardening what exists and extending the lifecycle, not inventing from scratch.

Now / Built

Core platform & resume engine

Professional & facility accounts, jobs, applications, network, messaging, timesheets, and the AI resume parsing/builder are already prototyped.

Next

Resume-upload onboarding & auto-anonymization

Let a professional drop in a resume to auto-fill their profile, and generate facility-ready anonymized resumes on demand — the highest-leverage use of the engine.

Next

Credential & compliance layer

License/certification expiry alerts and per-facility document checklists — the medical-specific features that separate Locumfy from a generic network.

Next

Get-paid flow

Turn approved timesheets into invoices and payouts, plus earnings and tax summaries — closing the loop from "apply" to "paid."

Later

Search, notifications & real-time messaging

Richer candidate search for employers, job-match alerts, and live chat to drive daily engagement.

Later

Scheduling, ratings & deeper analytics

Shift booking and availability, two-way reviews after assignments, and employer analytics such as time-to-fill and applicant funnels.

The headline for investors and partners: the hard research and prototyping is done. The remaining work funds hardening and go-to-market — extending an asset that already exists, not building one from zero.
