The Problem

Healthcare Talent is Fragmented

Medical professionals bounce between dozens of siloed job boards and staffing portals, while healthcare facilities struggle to find, verify, and engage qualified candidates.

🏥

Siloed Platforms

Nurses, therapists, and physicians each have separate portals — no unified identity or cross-discipline discovery for career-minded medical professionals.

📄

Resume Chaos

Resumes are manually reformatted — inconsistently and repeatedly — every time a candidate is submitted to a new hospital or facility. This wastes recruiter hours and introduces errors.

🔍

No Engagement Layer

LinkedIn is generic. Medical professionals have no career-specific social network for news, peer connections, and verified credentials in one place.

Roadmap: Payments — the Fintech Layer

Timesheets already flow through the platform. The next step closes the loop: approved timesheet → auto-generated invoice → same-week payout. A small take-rate on billed hours turns every completed shift into recurring transaction revenue — the model that made Clipboard Health a $1.3B company — while earnings dashboards and 1099 tax summaries lock in the contractor workforce.
