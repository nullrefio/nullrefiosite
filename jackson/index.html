The Problem

Healthcare Talent is Fragmented

Medical professionals bounce between dozens of siloed job boards and staffing portals, while healthcare facilities struggle to find, verify, and engage qualified candidates.

🏥

Siloed Platforms

Nurses, therapists, and physicians each have separate portals — no unified identity or cross-discipline discovery for career-minded medical professionals.

📄

Resume Chaos

Resumes are manually reformatted — inconsistently and repeatedly — every time a candidate is submitted to a new hospital or facility. This wastes recruiter hours and introduces errors.

🔍

No Engagement Layer

LinkedIn is generic. Medical professionals have no career-specific social network for news, peer connections, and verified credentials in one place.

The Solution

Introducing Locumfy

A LinkedIn-style platform purpose-built for healthcare — with verified credentials, AI-powered resume parsing, automated anonymization, job matching, built-in timesheets, and a professional social graph for medical careers.

🤝

Professional Network

Connect across disciplines. AI-suggested links based on shared employers, schools, and specialties.

💼

Unified Job Search

Internal and external positions across all Jackson brands surfaced in one intelligent feed.

🧾

Digital Timesheets

Mobile-first timesheet submission and approval — eliminating paper and manual billing delays.

🔬

Verified Data

NPI database integration auto-populates licenses, DEA, Medicare numbers, and state credentials.
