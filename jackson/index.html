+Locumfyfor healthcare Investor Introduction

A professional network built for healthcare.

Think LinkedIn — but purpose-built for nurses, therapists, physicians, and the facilities that hire them.

Thank you for taking a look at Locumfy. We're building the professional home for medical providers: a web and mobile platform that turns hundreds of thousands of existing resumes into rich, verified, connected profiles — then layers on the job search, networking, and workflow tools these professionals use every day. Rather than replacing today's profession-specific staffing brands, Locumfy unifies them under one engaging, branded experience for job search, connections, timesheets, and billing.

What makes it defensible is the data. Advanced resume parsing extracts skills, licenses, certifications, and specialties into searchable fields, matches them against public sources such as the NPI registry, and infers the professional relationships that power a real network — in early training data, roughly 660,000 connections surfaced from just 45,000 profiles. The same engine auto-formats and anonymizes resumes for third-party submission, removing a manual, error-prone step that recruiters do by hand today.

Key features

  • Curated provider profiles — parsed from real resumes and enriched with verifiable license, certification, and NPI data.
  • A genuine professional network — AI-scored connections, a specialty-specific feed, posts, and messaging.
  • Jobs & applications — providers discover and apply; facilities post, review candidates, and hire.
  • Timesheets & billing — work logged, approved, and paid inside the platform.
  • Standardized & anonymized resumes — one-click, submission-ready documents for facilities.
